Boone Support

Picture cards for your day, one at a time.

Write to us

Email support@getboone.app. A real person reads every message, usually within a few days.

In Boone you can also tap the ship's wheel at the top left, then Send feedback. That fills in which version of Boone you have.

Boone isn't a crisis service. If you're in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, in the US) or call 911.

Common questions

How many times can I use Chart Steps?

5 times a day for free. Your free uses come back when your day starts again. Cards with ready-made steps don't use any.

The steps were wrong or upsetting.

Under the steps, tap Report these steps. Your email app opens with the card's name and the steps, so you can tell us what went wrong. You can also change or delete any step yourself.

How do I move my cards to a new phone?

On your old phone, tap the ship's wheel, then Save a backup file, and put it somewhere your new phone can reach, like iCloud Drive or Google Drive. On the new phone, tap the ship's wheel, then Open a backup file.

Where are my cards kept?

Only on your phone. Boone has no accounts, so we can't see your cards or get them back for you. Your phone's own backup includes them if you have it turned on.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything Boone saved on your phone goes with it. We don't keep your cards, so there's nothing on our side to delete. Our Privacy Policy has the details.
