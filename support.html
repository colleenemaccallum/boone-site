Write to Us

Email support@getboone.app. A real person reads every message, usually within a few days.

In Boone you can also open Settings and tap Send Feedback. That fills in which version of Boone you have. To open Settings, tap the harbor picture at the top of Today, then the ship's wheel at the top right.

Boone isn't a crisis service. If you're in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, in the US) or call 911.

Common Questions

What Is Help Now?

Tap Help at the bottom left of the app. It has A Quiet Minute, where you breathe with the tide, buttons to call or text 988, and Communication Cards, with words you can show someone. The 988 buttons only open your phone's own dialer or messages. Boone doesn't send anything or keep a record.

How Many Times Can I Use Chart Steps and Brain Dump?

5 times a day for free, shared between the two. Your free uses come back when your day starts again. Tasks with ready-made steps don't use any. Tapping Smaller on one step uses one, and so does each Sort Into Tasks in Brain Dump.

The Steps Were Wrong or Upsetting.

Under the steps, tap Report these steps by email. Your email app opens with the task's name and the steps, so you can tell us what went wrong. You can also change or delete any step yourself.

How Do I Move My Tasks to a New Phone?

On your old phone, open Settings, then tap Save a backup file, and put it somewhere your new phone can reach, like iCloud Drive or Google Drive. On the new phone, tap New Phone? Open a Backup File on the first screen. Or, later, open Settings, then tap Open a backup file.

Where Are My Tasks Kept?

Only on your phone. Boone has no accounts, so we can't see your tasks or get them back for you. Your phone's own backup includes them if you have it turned on.

How Do I Delete My Data?

Delete the app. Everything Boone saved on your phone goes with it. We don't keep your tasks, so there's nothing on our side to delete. Our Privacy Policy has the details.
