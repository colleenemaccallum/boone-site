Boone Support

Picture cards for your day, one at a time.

Write to Us

Email support@getboone.app. A real person reads every message, usually within a few days.

In Boone you can also open the Binder and tap the ship's wheel at the right end of its tabs, then Send feedback. That fills in which version of Boone you have.

Boone isn't a crisis service. If you're in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, in the US) or call 911.

Common Questions

What Is Help Now?

Tap Help Now at the bottom left of the app. It has A Quiet Minute, where you breathe with the tide, buttons to call or text 988, and Can't Talk Right Now, with words you can show someone. The 988 buttons only open your phone's own dialer or messages. Boone doesn't send anything or keep a record.

How Many Times Can I Use Chart Steps?

5 times a day for free. Your free uses come back when your day starts again. Cards with ready-made steps don't use any. Tapping Smaller on one step uses one too.

The Steps Were Wrong or Upsetting.

Under the steps, tap Report these steps by email. Your email app opens with the card's name and the steps, so you can tell us what went wrong. You can also change or delete any step yourself.

How Do I Move My Cards to a New Phone?

On your old phone, open the Binder, tap the ship's wheel, then Save a backup file, and put it somewhere your new phone can reach, like iCloud Drive or Google Drive. On the new phone, tap New Phone? Open a Backup File on the first screen. Or, later, tap the ship's wheel in the Binder, then Open a backup file.

Where Are My Cards Kept?

Only on your phone. Boone has no accounts, so we can't see your cards or get them back for you. Your phone's own backup includes them if you have it turned on.

How Do I Delete My Data?

Delete the app. Everything Boone saved on your phone goes with it. We don't keep your cards, so there's nothing on our side to delete. Our Privacy Policy has the details.
