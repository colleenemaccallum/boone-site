Meet Boone.

A walrus of few words.

Boone works on the dock while you work through your cards. He nods when you finish one and naps when it gets late. He never nags and never needs looking after.

If you'd rather work alone, one switch sends him home.

Stuck on a big one?

One tap on Break it down turns a card like Clean Kitchen into small steps you can actually start. Change the steps before you keep them.

Nothing to lose.

No streaks, and nothing ever turns red. Each day you open Boone, a sticker is waiting. Miss a day and nothing happens.
