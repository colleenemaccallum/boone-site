For the cards you keep putting off.

Chart Steps splits a big task into small steps.

Tap me Take Out Trash
Tie up the trash bag
Carry it to the outside bin
Put a new bag in the can

That little map sits in the corner of every card. It's called Chart Steps. Each step becomes its own card with its own picture, so you only ever look at the next small thing.

Still too big? Tap Tiny steps. Too many? Tap Bigger steps. Change or remove any step you like. When the last one is done, the whole card counts.

Everyday cards have steps ready, even offline. For anything else, an AI helper writes the steps (it asks you first), and you get a few free every day.
