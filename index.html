That little buoy sits in the corner of every card. It's called Break it down. Each step becomes its own card with its own picture, so you only ever look at the next small thing.

Still too big? Tap Tiny steps. Too many? Tap Bigger steps. Change or remove any step you like. When the last one is done, the whole card counts.

Everyday cards have steps ready, even offline. For anything else, an AI helper writes the steps (it asks you first), and you get a few free every day.

Why Boone?

Boone is for days when there's so much to do and it's still hard to start. It helps you figure out what to do next, especially when you're overwhelmed and everyone needs something from you.
