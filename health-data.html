Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This is Boone's Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy under Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law and similar laws. Boone is run by Colleen MacCallum, Naples, Florida, United States.

These laws define "consumer health data" very broadly. A card name like "Take medication" or "Therapy appointment" could count.

  • What we collect: only card names you choose to send with Chart Steps. Boone doesn't ask about diagnoses, conditions or medications. Using Boone doesn't tell us that you have any condition.
  • Where it comes from: you, when you tap Chart Steps.
  • Why: only to suggest steps for that card.
  • Who receives it: Anthropic, as our service provider, only to write the steps. Our hosting and database providers handle the request but don't keep the card text. We don't sell it or share it for any other purpose.
  • Your consent: we ask before the first Chart Steps, and you can say Not now.
  • Your rights: you can ask what consumer health data we have about you, ask us to delete it, and withdraw consent by not using Chart Steps. Because we don't keep card text, there's normally nothing for us to hand over or delete, but write to support@getboone.app and we'll confirm within 45 days. If we turn down a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and if you're unhappy with the result you can contact your state Attorney General.
