Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This is Boone's Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy under Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law and similar laws. Boone is run by Colleen MacCallum, Naples, Florida, United States.

These laws define "consumer health data" very broadly. A task name like "Take Medication" or "Therapy Appointment" could count, and so could the words in a brain dump.

  • What we collect: only task and step names you choose to send with Chart Steps, and the words you choose to send with Sort Into Tasks in Brain Dump. Boone doesn't ask about diagnoses, conditions or medications. Using Boone doesn't tell us that you have any condition.
  • Where it comes from: you, when you tap Chart Steps or Sort Into Tasks.
  • Why: only to suggest steps for that task, or to sort your brain dump into tasks.
  • Who receives it: Anthropic, as our service provider, only to write the steps or sort the tasks. Our hosting and database providers handle the request but don't keep the text. We don't sell it or share it for any other purpose.
  • Your consent: we ask before the first Chart Steps or Sort Into Tasks, and you can say Not Now.
  • Your rights: you can ask what consumer health data we have about you, ask us to delete it, and withdraw consent by not using Chart Steps or Brain Dump, or by turning off Send Task Names to the AI Helper in Settings. Because we don't keep the text, there's normally nothing for us to hand over or delete, but write to support@getboone.app and we'll confirm within 45 days. If we turn down a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and if you're unhappy with the result you can contact your state Attorney General.
